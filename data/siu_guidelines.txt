SPECIAL INVESTIGATION UNIT (SIU) GUIDELINES
Synthetic Training Document — Aspire Claims Academy
====================================================

1. PURPOSE
These guidelines help claim handlers identify potential fraud indicators and
determine when a claim should be referred to the Special Investigation Unit (SIU).

2. COMMON FRAUD INDICATORS
- Staged accident: Collision appears intentionally caused or coordinated.
- Inconsistent statements: Insured account differs across interviews or from witnesses.
- Suspicious loss timing: Loss occurs shortly after policy inception or coverage change.
- Prior claims: Repeated similar losses across short time periods.
- Excessive damages: Claimed damage exceeds what the described event would cause.
- Late reporting: Unexplained delay between loss date and claim notice.
- Witness anomalies: No independent witnesses, or witness contradicts physical evidence.
- Damage pattern inconsistency: Physical damage does not match reported impact angle.

3. REFERRAL CRITERIA
Refer to SIU when one or more of the following apply:
A. Clear evidence of staged accident indicators.
B. Material inconsistent statements between insured and witness.
C. Suspicious loss timing combined with other red flags.
D. Prior claims history suggesting a pattern of abuse.
E. Excessive damages unsupported by photos or repair estimates.
F. Handler or adjuster has a reasonable suspicion of fraud.

4. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
- Record all statements verbatim where possible.
- Preserve photos, police reports, and repair estimates.
- Note the specific fraud indicators observed.
- Do not accuse the claimant; document facts and referral reason only.

5. HANDLER ACTIONS BEFORE REFERRAL
1. Complete initial claim intake.
2. Compare insured statement to witness statement.
3. Review prior claims on the same policy or claimant.
4. Check loss timing relative to policy effective date.
5. Submit SIU referral with supporting evidence.

6. KEY TERMS FOR INVESTIGATORS
staged accident | inconsistent statements | suspicious loss timing |
prior claims | excessive damages | fraud indicators | referral criteria |
rear-end collision | witness statement | policy inception

7. EXAMPLE REFERRAL LANGUAGE
"Claim referred to SIU due to inconsistent statements between the insured and
an independent witness, combined with damage inconsistent with a low-speed
rear-end collision."
